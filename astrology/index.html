The rising sign

How you meet the world.

Your chart, accurately

How to find your
Moon & rising.

There is no reliable shortcut from your Sun sign. Use a natal-chart calculator with an ephemeris and historical time-zone data, then read the Moon and Ascendant labels.

Open Astro.com’s chart tools →

An independent third-party site. Review its privacy terms before entering birth information. Crystaleye does not calculate or collect your birth details here.

  1. Gather your birth details

    Use your full birth date, recorded local birth time, and birth city or coordinates. A birth record is preferable to a family estimate. Check whether the time was a.m. or p.m.

  2. Set the chart correctly

    Use the tropical zodiac to match these profiles. Confirm the location and historical time zone, including daylight saving where applicable; a reputable calculator converts the recorded time to UTC.

  3. Read your Moon placement

    The calculator looks up the Moon’s zodiac position at that instant. The Moon changes signs roughly every two to three days, so exact birth time matters near a boundary. Record the sign beside the Moon symbol.

  4. Read your Ascendant

    The calculator uses birth time and geographical location to find the eastern horizon’s intersection with the ecliptic. Find “ASC” or “Ascendant.” Rising signs change roughly every two hours on average, with substantial variation by latitude and sign.

What if I do not know my birth time?

A noon chart does not give a reliable rising sign. Leave the Ascendant unknown instead of guessing. For the Moon, compare its position across the whole possible birth-time range: if it changes signs, both are possible until you have better time information. Near a Sun-sign boundary, check the exact birth year and time as well. If the recorded time is uncertain, allow that uncertainty in the interpretation.

Are the house associations on these pages my actual houses?

No. The numbered house associations in the profiles are a common modern symbolic analogy between the twelve signs and twelve houses. Signs and houses are distinct; your actual house placements depend on your natal chart and chosen house system. A rising-sign description here does not automatically place every planet in a particular house.

Sample edition · example Sun Aries, Moon Cancer, rising Libra

A little space for yourself.

Sun · Aries

A conversation may benefit from your courage, but it also needs your attention. Say the honest thing without racing to the final word. If you feel yourself turning a difference of opinion into a competition, ask one sincere question before responding. Your initiative becomes more useful when another person has room to contribute.

Moon · Cancer

Ask directly for the care or reassurance you need instead of hoping someone decodes your silence. A request gives another person a fair chance to respond. Notice whether an old disappointment is adding weight to the present moment. You can protect your tenderness while checking what is actually happening now.

Rising · Libra

Name what you want before checking how everyone else feels about it. You can be considerate without making your own view the last item on the agenda. A clear preference gives people something honest to work with. The aim is a fair exchange, not a flawless performance of being easy to please.

Take what resonates. Leave room for your own experience.
