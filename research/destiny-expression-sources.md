# Destiny / Expression — Research and synthesis notes

Access date: **2026-11-13 (America/Vancouver)**. Public pages were retrieved live over HTTPS and their full relevant article text was read. Search listings were used to locate articles, not as substitutes for reading them.

## Scope and source families

Three publishers provide substantive Expression-specific profiles for every retained result: Hans Decoz / World Numerology, Felicia Bender, and Numerology.com. Sarah Yip is a fourth substantive publisher used only for a written name-convention contrast. Multiple pages from one publisher count as one family. Common themes are not independent empirical validation, and no independence of these traditions or publishers is assumed.

## Valid results and selected calculation

The selected Western Pythagorean method yields 1–9, 11/2, 22/4, and 33/6. D0 explicitly preserves 11, 22, 33 at the completed name-part and final reduction stages. Bender has separate Expression profiles for these results, and N explicitly lists the same range. Zero is not a retained result for a nonempty alphabetic name. 44 reduces to 8 here. Karmic Debt compounds are not additional profile results in this guide.

Use every birth-name part, all letters, values A–I = 1–9 repeating through Z. Reduce each name-part sum first, preserving 11/22/33, then combine and reduce with the same exceptions. Do not stop on an arbitrary running subtotal. Punctuation is not assigned a value. A transliteration must be documented for a name outside this letter table; there is no claim of a universal transliteration.

## Consequential differences

- **Reduction order:** D0 explicitly rejects combining all raw letters; BM instructs name-part reduction. N demonstrates a combined raw-letter sum. Invented name Anna Alice: Anna 12 → 3; Alice 21 → 3; name-part result 6 versus raw total 33. Root congruence does not preserve master-number status.
- **Birth-certificate error:** BM insists on the original recorded spelling, even erroneous. D0 permits the intended original spelling if parents rejected the clerical error and used that name.
- **Current name:** D0 and BM distinguish foundational birth-name numbers from current-name minor overlays. Y discusses a current legal name as a Destiny number. That page establishes a contrast of scope, not a full alternative calculation standard.
- **Meaning of the position:** D0 emphasizes birth-name capacities and potential; B0 emphasizes the manner in which a Life Path purpose is expressed. This guide synthesizes capacities and expression without relabeling Life Path themes.
- **33 compared with 6:** D33 largely repeats 6 material, then adds broader service and an age/event claim. B33 foregrounds doubled-3 communication and teaching; N likewise stresses guidance and creative communication. This guide uses a distinct 33/6 profile but rejects the age/event prediction.
- **11 and 22:** Some source language claims psychic perception or exceptional, limitless achievement. The guide keeps symbolic inspiration and practical organization while excluding paranormal certainty, destiny pressure, and rankings of worth.

## Project calculator distinction

Inspected numerology-calculator/app.js without editing it. `nameNumbers` reduces consonant and vowel sums per word, combines each set into Personality and Soul, and then reduces Personality + Soul to obtain Destiny. That is not the direct all-letter, name-part calculation selected for this guide. Invented full name Thomas Ann: direct name parts 22 + 11 = 33/6; calculator consonants reduce to Personality 7, vowels to Soul 8, then Destiny 15 → 6. These examples were independently recalculated. No calculator behavior was changed. Current-name defaults and clamp-to-zero Challenge rules were not researched or altered as part of this topic.

## Editorial boundaries and coverage gaps

All core and difficulty paragraphs synthesize position-specific research. Descriptive subtitles and all final constructive paragraphs are editorial. Specific exercises (feedback, bounded projects, agreements, evidence checks, delegation) apply the themes; they are not presented as tested interventions or direct quotations. Per-profile source IDs identify support, not empirical proof.

No missing result coverage remains within the selected twelve-result convention: each has at least two detailed, position-specific interpretations plus N. Coverage of 33 as something distinct from 6 is less consistent across sources, which is disclosed. Non-Latin naming conventions and unknown original birth names have no universal resolution in this research.

Ann Perry’s homepage returned HTTP 202 with a short challenge response, not a substantive article. It was not counted as a source. Initial requests without a browser-style User-Agent received some 403 responses; normal public-page requests with Mozilla/5.0 returned HTTP 200. An initial obsolete Numerology.com URL returned 404; the exact article was found through the site navigation. No TLS verification, network policy, authentication, or access challenge was bypassed. Only accessible public written material was used. Y’s embedded video and linked case-study articles were not reviewed.
