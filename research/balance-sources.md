Accessed **November 13, 2026 (America/Vancouver)**. Research for this topic was performed live over HTTPS. All relevant cited article content was read, including the nine Balance sections and the supplementary general-number and Life Path discussions. Search listings were used for discovery, not as evidence for deep interpretation.

## Scope and source-family accounting

Eleven cited articles come from three publisher families: Hans Decoz / World Numerology, Numerology.com, and Felicia Bender. Only Decoz supplies direct Balance position evidence in the accessible material. Numerology.com supplies general numeric definitions, strengths and weaknesses. Bender’s How To Say “NO” essay explicitly discusses Life Path boundary-setting, not Balance. These limitations appear in the PDF methodology, every result’s evidence note, structured source roles and this file.

Every result compares Decoz’s Balance-specific recommendation with the corresponding general-number interpretation and a clearly labeled Life Path comparison. These are at least two relevant thematic interpretations, not two independently researched Balance profiles. Multiple pages from one publisher are one source family. Agreement about symbols does not establish independent empirical evidence or scientific validity.

## Valid result range established before writing

D explicitly says to add initials of the full birth name and reduce to one digit, including master totals. Its worked example Thomas John Hancock gives T2 + J1 + H8 = 11 → 2, and it supplies nine results, 1–9. This guide follows that declared convention. It does not generalize a master-retention rule from the previous name guides to Balance. It does not assert that every school uses the same procedure.

Zero is not an interpreted result for a valid nonempty alphabetic name: each initial contributes a positive 1–9 value. Challenge 0 conventions do not apply here. No Balance 11/22/33 profiles are invented from general master symbolism. The calculator’s possible outputs are separately mapped to the guide roots in calculator_compatibility.

## Calculation and project compatibility

- **Name source:** full birth name, not current name or birth date. Use initials of the words in the project’s birth first/middle/last fields; every entered middle-name word is included. D’s example uses one first, middle and last initial; the exact word-token rule for compound names is a project convention and should be recorded, not called universal.
- **Letter values:** repeating Pythagorean A–Z = 1–9. Every initial counts, whether vowel or consonant. There is no Y/W vowel-selection question for Balance.
- **Tokenization:** the existing calculator splits words at whitespace and removes apostrophes and hyphens inside each word. A hyphenated word therefore contributes one initial. It strips combining accents and restricts input to Latin A–Z after normalization. No universal transliteration or compound-name convention is inferred.
- **Selected guide reduction:** add initial values, repeatedly sum decimal digits until 1–9; reduce master totals too.
- **Existing calculator:** nameNumbers sums initial values, then uses reduceNumber, which preserves 11, 22, 33. This differs from D. The source and behavior were inspected without editing.
- **Mapping:** app 11 → guide 2; app 22 → guide 4; app 33 → guide 6. Other final 1–9 values agree. This mapping is explanatory, not a silent software change.
- **Verified examples:** Thomas John Hancock = 2+1+8 = 11 → guide 2, app 11. Invented Ivy Ian Dana = 9+9+4 = 22 → guide 4, app 22. Invented Ivy Ian Isaac Ford = 9+9+9+6 = 33 → guide 6, app 33. Invented Ada Ben Cora = 1+2+3 = 6 for both. The four app results were obtained by evaluating the unmodified nameNumbers function; final single-digit reductions were independently checked.
- **No calculator modification:** the master-preserving Balance formula and the unrelated clamp-to-zero Challenge behavior remain intact.

## Editorial boundaries

Profiles are original, response-focused syntheses of approximately 200 words each. They describe a constructive theme when facing a difficulty, rather than a fixed personality, a hidden motive, a life cycle, or an assertion about the reader’s actual coping style. Subtitles, ordinary examples, and practical final paragraphs are editorial. Several tensions in the first two paragraphs are also editorial applications; they are not attributed as verbatim source claims.

General number symbolism supplements the Balance reading rather than replacing it. For example, N1 emphasizes self-sufficiency, while D’s Balance 1 explicitly asks for openness to help; D’s position-specific corrective takes priority. Bender’s Life Path observations about saying no are not relabeled Balance descriptions and are not used to calculate the result.

D contains advice that is not adopted literally: Balance 6 asserts the reader helped create the situation; 5 discusses escaping into food/alcohol/drugs; 7 asks the reader to get past emotion; several sections suggest a solution is assured. The guide excludes blanket blame, addiction/health inferences, dismissal of feelings, and guaranteed outcomes. Responsibility is limited to a reasonable next action within one’s control. No number establishes that suffering or mistreatment was caused, deserved, necessary, or should be endured.

## Discovery, access limits and remaining gaps

The direct D page was located through World Numerology’s Personality/Soul Urge navigation and confirmed in its live sitemap. Two initially assumed Balance paths returned 404 and were not used. Numerology.com’s assumed Balance URL returned 404; its live sitemap had no Balance entry. Its general number articles were located through internal numeric-meaning links and read freshly for this topic.

Bender’s exact-phrase “balance number” search returned zero results. Her broad search produced material about other positions and ordinary balance. Sarah Yip’s exact-phrase search returned no matching article. Bender’s Life Path boundary-setting essay was followed from the broad search and read in full. Her Odd & Even Numbers article was also read during discovery but is not cited as Balance evidence; its general introvert/extrovert classification and reported Juno Jordan material do not supply a Balance method or result series. Juno Jordan’s books were not independently consulted.

Requests to https://numerologist.com/ and https://www.numerologymeaning.com/ were denied by the configured egress proxy with tunnel HTTP 403. No content from those sites was read or counted. Network policy was not bypassed, and configuration was not changed. The current allowlist limits the accessible research destinations. This is a bounded search, not a finding that no further Balance sources exist.

A second accessible Balance-specific publisher remains a material gap. General symbolism and a Life Path comparison do not erase that limitation. No corroborating alternate Balance formula is claimed; the consequential documented disagreement is between D’s published one-digit method and the project’s master-preserving implementation. Broader school-to-school variation was not verified. Raw HTML caches and page renders remain outside the repository.
