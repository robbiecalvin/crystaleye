NUMEROLOGY CALCULATOR

Keep index.html, styles.css, and app.js together in one folder.
Double-click index.html to open the calculator in your browser.
No installation, internet connection, or Google Sheets required.

Use the alternate-name checkbox to enter a current name. When unchecked, birth names are used for Minor numbers.
Challenge results below zero become zero; the Third Challenge uses the corrected First and Second Challenges.
