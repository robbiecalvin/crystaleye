How it is calculated

  1. Reduce the birth month, day, and four-digit year separately, keeping 11, 22, and 33 when reached.
  2. Add those three reduced components.
  3. Reduce the combined total, again preserving a final 11, 22, or 33.
Conventions worth knowing

Example: October 22, 1980 → month 10→1; day 22 stays 22; year 1980→18→9. Then 1+22+9=32→5. This component method matches the existing calculator; other schools use different procedures.
