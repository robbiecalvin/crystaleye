How it is calculated

  1. For this app, reduce the month, day, and year with 11, 22, and 33 retained.
  2. Calculate First Challenge minus Second Challenge.
  3. If the difference is negative, use zero. Keep the resulting whole number without further reduction.
Conventions worth knowing

This is Crystaleye’s custom calculator rule. Other numerology methods often use absolute differences after full single-digit reduction. Retaining masters can make this app’s unreduced differences exceed 9. The 0–9 routes and reserved master routes do not substitute a generic number meaning for an unpublished Challenge interpretation.
