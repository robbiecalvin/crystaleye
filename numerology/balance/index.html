How it is calculated

  1. Take the first letter of each word in the full birth name, including entered middle-name words.
  2. Add the Pythagorean values of those initials.
  3. For the completed guide, reduce fully to 1–9, including 11→2, 22→4, and 33→6.
Conventions worth knowing

The established full calculator keeps 11, 22, and 33 for Balance. Use guide 2 for app 11, guide 4 for app 22, and guide 6 for app 33. These routes explain the mapping; they do not invent separate master Balance profiles.
