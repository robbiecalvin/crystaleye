How it is calculated

  1. Take the birth day.
  2. Reduce by adding digits until a single digit or retained master 11, 22, or 33 is reached.
  3. Use that result for this cycle position; do not combine it with the other cycle values.
Conventions worth knowing

This is the existing calculator’s component rule. The first cycle comes from month, the second from day, and the third from year. Result availability reflects the calculator’s current 1800–2026 birth-year range; no cycle ages are assigned here.
