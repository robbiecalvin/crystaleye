Meet Robbie

The person behind The Crystal Eye

Robbie

I’m a creator at heart—drawn to stories, symbols, patterns, and the meaning people find in them. Through love, loss, and some difficult new beginnings, that curiosity became more personal. I started asking deeper questions: What am I overlooking? What am I holding onto? What could change if I listened to myself?

Tarot, numerology, and astrology gave those questions a language. They became a meeting place for my creativity, intuition, and desire to help people feel understood.

That’s the spirit behind The Crystal Eye: a little mystery, a lot of colour, and room for honest reflection. My approach is compassionate, direct, and grounded in the belief that a reading should leave you feeling more connected to your own choices.

I’m still writing my story. I’d be honoured to help you explore yours.
